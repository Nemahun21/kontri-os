Kontri‑Os: The Cultural Companion

A quest-based learning adventure that brings culture, history, and languages to your fingertips. Explore, discover, and stay connected to home. Created by nemahun21.

Our Mission

Sierra Leone's culture is rich, but its digital presence is small. We built Kontrios(The Country's House) to bridge that gap. Our mission is to create a vibrant, digital ecosystem where learning about our heritage is not just educational, but genuinely engaging. It's a digital home built for learning, celebrating, and preserving our heritage for generations to come.

— nemahun21 initiative

How It Works

Three simple steps to start your journey.

Choose your path

Pick a category – History, Food, Sports,Places, or Languages – and start at level 1.

Quest & Discover

Move through levels at your own pace. Each lesson teaches before the quests begin.

Grow & Progress

Track your score, build streaks, and unlock new levels as you learn.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Kontri-Os is completely free to download and use.

What platforms are supported?

Kontri-Os runs on Android and in the browser. You can also download the APK directly from this site for Android devices.

Who created Kontri-Os?

Kontri-Os is a product of nemahun21, a startup building digital tools to meet Sierra Leonean context.

How can I contribute?

We are building a place where the community can contribute facts and knowledge. Reach out if you would like to help preserve and share Sierra Leonean heritage.

The Long‑Term Vision

We aim to become a hub for knowledge: user-created facts, school partnerships, and a community where learning about home is celebrated. Join us on this journey.
